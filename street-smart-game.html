An interactive game for parents & kids to practice real safety skills together — because brave kids know what to do.

👨‍👩‍👧 Note for Parents

This game is designed for you to play together with your child. It teaches the NCMEC's safety framework through scenarios and practice exercises. After some rounds, we'll ask you and your child to stand up and practice together — saying "NO" loudly, running to safety, and yelling for help. This builds real muscle memory. The tone is empowering, not scary — we're raising brave kids, not fearful ones.
